# Subtitle timeline preservation — 2026-09-30

## Problem and behavior

Issue #322 isolates a strict MP4 timed-text failure to simultaneous SRT cues, independently
of video encoding or hardware decoding. A freely generated fixture has two English cues at
one second, overlapping Japanese cues at four/five seconds, and one compatible French cue.

The planner reads the kept tracks' absolute packet indexes and timestamps. Conflicting or
unproven timing selects Matroska; filtering to French still selects MP4. Text and timing are
preserved without dropping cues, flattening durations or disabling strict errors. An unreadable
source probe stops the job before encoding. No verification thresholds or model changed.

## Second-order review

- Language rules are applied before deciding container compatibility. Removed tracks neither
  trigger fallback nor change kept output stream order.
- Separate tracks can have cues at the same time; only conflicts within a kept track matter.
- Adjacent cues and sub-microsecond boundary rounding remain compatible. Missing/non-finite
  times, non-positive durations, reordered cues and missing packet evidence are conservative.
- Fresh absolute indexes are required rather than guessed from subtitle-relative positions.
- Numeric timing is streamed without cue payloads or video/audio decoding. A proved conflict
  stops the scan; cancellation and other exits stop the owned process.
- Ordinary jobs without affected subtitles retain their existing probe path. Existing protocol
  4 workers accept the resulting commands; no worker update or database migration is required.
- Failed production jobs and originals were not retried or modified by acceptance tests.

Final-image CI now runs both subtitle cases and retains its report. CI, publication and
Git-backed deployment must complete before treating this as a live deployed fix.
